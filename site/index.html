M2 Solution

About

Two people. One studio.

M2 Solution is Mathis Côté and Mathieu Laureti. We design and build web solutions for businesses that need a presence that is clear, considered, and built to last.

Based in Québec. We work in French and English — the way your clients actually speak.

What we do

Web work that serves the business.

  • Sites

    A public face that explains who you are, what you offer, and why it matters — in the language of your clients.

  • Tools

    Pages and systems that support how you actually work, instead of adding another layer to manage.

  • Care

    We stay with the work after launch: updates, improvements, and the details that keep things running.

Who it’s for

Businesses that want a site that works, not a brochure that ages.

  • Local businesses

    Shops, services, and studios that need to be found, understood, and contacted without friction.

  • Growing teams

    Companies that have outgrown a template and need something that matches how they sell and operate.

  • Bilingual Québec

    Work that has to hold up in French and English, on the same footing — not a translation tacked on at the end.

How we work

Listen, build, stay with it.

  1. Listen

    We start with the business: who you serve, what has to be clear, and what can wait. No deck before the problem is named.

  2. Build

    Design and development together, in the open. You see the work as it takes shape, not only at the end.

  3. Launch

    We put it live, set the domain and mail, and keep a line open for what comes next.

Questions

The usual ones.

How do we start?

Write to contact@m2solution.ca with a few lines on the business and what you need. We answer, then we talk.

How long does a site take?

It depends on the scope. A clear one-pager can move quickly; a larger site takes the time the content and structure need. We’ll say so before we begin.

Do you work in French and English?

Yes. The studio is bilingual. The site you’re reading is the proof.

Do you host and look after the site?

Yes. We can launch it, point the domain, and stay on for updates so you’re not left with a file and a login.
